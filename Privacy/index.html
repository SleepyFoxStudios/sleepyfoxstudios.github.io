PRIVACY POLICY: Picnic Shuffle

Effective date: October 15, 2026

1. Who we are

Picnic Shuffle (“the app”) is a puzzle game made by Sleepy Fox Studios (“we”, “us”). If you have questions about this policy, email us at charlie.low@sleepy-fox-studios.com.

2. The short version

  • We don’t collect your personal information. The app has no accounts and no sign-in, and we don’t run servers that receive your data.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some data from your device to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. Section 4 explains what it collects.
  • You choose. Apple’s tracking prompt and Google’s consent message let you decide how your data is used for ads. You can change those choices at any time.
  • Your game progress stays on your device.

3. Information we collect

We, Sleepy Fox Studios, don’t collect, receive, store, or sell any personal information from the app. Your progress, settings, and your “No Ads” purchase status are saved only on your device (see Section 7).

If you email us, we’ll receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you, and we delete it once your question is resolved unless we need to keep it to meet a legal obligation.

4. Information collected by Google AdMob

The app uses Google AdMob, operated by Google LLC (or Google Ireland Ltd. for users in the EEA and UK), to show interstitial ads between levels and optional rewarded ads (for example, watching an ad to get a hint or +5 moves). When ads load, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect: - Device identifiers. This includes the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow tracking in Apple’s prompt. It can also include app- or developer-scoped identifiers. - IP address. It can be used to estimate your general (coarse) location, such as your country or city. The app doesn’t use GPS or precise location. - Ad and usage data. For example, which ads you saw or tapped, video views, and app launches. - Diagnostic data. For example, crash logs and performance data such as launch time.

Google uses this data to show ads and measure them, for analytics, and to detect fraud and abuse. When you’ve given permission, it can also personalize ads. Google handles this data under its own policies. See “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services”: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

We require our advertising partner to protect your data at least as well as this policy describes, and we only work with partners that commit to doing so.

5. Your choices: tracking and ad consent

  • Apple’s tracking prompt (App Tracking Transparency). On iOS, the app asks whether it may track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, Google won’t receive your IDFA and you’ll still see ads, but they’ll be less personalized. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or in Settings > Picnic Shuffle.
  • Google’s consent message (EEA, UK, Switzerland). In these regions, a Google consent message asks for your choices before personalized ads or certain data uses happen. You can accept, reject, or manage the options.
  • US state privacy choices. In some US states, the same Google message lets you opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of your personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Changing your mind. Open Settings > Privacy options in the app. Where the law requires it, this button reopens Google’s message so you can review or withdraw your choices.
  • Rewards never depend on these choices. Rewarded ads, hints, and +5 moves work the same whether or not you allow tracking or personalized ads.

6. Purchases

The “No Ads” upgrade is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we never see or receive your card or payment details. The app gets only a confirmation from Apple that the purchase is valid, and it saves that on your device so ads stay off. To restore the purchase on a new device, use Restore Purchases in Settings. Apple’s handling of your payment is covered by Apple’s privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

7. Game saves stored on your device

Your progress, stars, coins, settings, and No Ads status are stored locally on your device using the game engine’s standard storage. They aren’t sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data. Because we don’t have a copy, we can’t restore it if it’s lost.

8. Children

Picnic Shuffle is a general-audience game. It isn’t directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live), and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us at charlie.low@sleepy-fox-studios.com and we’ll delete it. If we learn that the app is being used by a child under 13 in a way that sends personal information to our ad partner, we’ll take reasonable steps to stop that. For example, we can set ad requests to Google’s child-directed treatment (non-personalized, family-safe ads).

9. Your rights

EEA, UK, and Switzerland. You have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent doesn’t affect processing that happened before. Personalized advertising and the storing or reading of information on your device rely on your consent, which you give or refuse in Google’s consent message. Some ad measurement and fraud prevention may rely on legitimate interests, as described in Google’s message. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

US state residents (for example, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, and other states with privacy laws). Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete or correct it, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted (cross-context behavioral) advertising. We don’t sell personal information for money and we don’t collect it ourselves. Even so, allowing Google to use device identifiers for personalized ads may count as “sharing” or “selling” under some state laws. To opt out, choose Ask App Not to Track, use Settings > Privacy options in the app, or email us. We’ll honor opt-out signals where the law requires it, and we won’t treat you differently for using your rights.

Because we don’t hold your data, most requests about ad data are best handled by Google: https://myadcenter.google.com and https://policies.google.com/privacy. We’ll still help with any request sent to charlie.low@sleepy-fox-studios.com.

10. How long data is kept

We don’t keep app data. Emails you send us are kept only as long as we need them to answer you. Google keeps the data it collects under its own retention policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention.

11. Security

We don’t store your data on our own systems, so there’s nothing on our side for anyone to breach. Data sent to Google is transmitted using industry-standard encryption. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your device’s software up to date.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the effective date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, such as adding a new data partner, we’ll also give notice in the app or the App Store release notes.
